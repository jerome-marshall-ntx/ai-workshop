# Session 1 — Foundations of AI for Engineers

Even if you already use AI tools daily, understanding the "why" behind these systems helps you get a lot more out of them.

We'll walk through:

- **How AI has evolved** — from rule-based systems to today's LLMs
- **What LLMs are good at** — and where they still fall short
- **How LLMs work behind the scenes** — tokens, context windows, attention
- **Prompting techniques** that actually improve output quality
- **Real-world examples** of AI in engineering workflows

The goal: build a shared mental model so we're all on the same page before we get into the hands-on stuff.

---

# Session 2 — Advanced Cursor: Context Engineering

This is where it gets practical. Session 2 is about making AI work in real, complex codebases — not just toy projects.

We'll cover:

- **Why AI falls apart on large codebases** — and what to do about it
- **Context engineering** — the single most important skill for AI-assisted development
- **The smart zone vs the dumb zone** — how to stay where AI performs best
- **A complete workflow** — Research, Plan, Implement
- **Rules, commands, and skills** — teaching Cursor about your project
- **Live demos** — real examples, not slides
