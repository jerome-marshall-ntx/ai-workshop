Section 2
Lost in the Middle
Liu et al., Stanford, 2023 — "How Language Models Use Long Contexts"
Accuracy Position of relevant information in context
Beginning · Middle (info gets lost) · End
The U-shaped curve: AI retrieves information well from the start and end of context, but misses information placed in the middle. Tested across GPT-3.5-Turbo, Claude, MPT-30B, and LongChat. This is why the "middle" of long conversations becomes unreliable.

What this means: When AI says "You're absolutely right" and changes its answer, it's sycophancy — prioritizing agreement over correctness. This happens 58% of the time across major models and persists 78.5% of the time. It's driven by RLHF training where human raters prefer agreeable-sounding responses.
